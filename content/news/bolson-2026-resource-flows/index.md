Our paper “Resource flows in the UK’s foundation industries” has been published in *Resources, Conservation and Recycling*, with José Azevedo and Jonathan Cullen.

The UK foundation industries supply essential materials but remain difficult to decarbonise because of high-temperature processing and process emissions.
