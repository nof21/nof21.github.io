Our paper “Navigating Carbon Accounting in Manufacturing From Compliance to Decarbonisation” has been published in *Business Strategy and the Environment*, with Alexandra Rosioru, Marie Wells and Justyna Rybicka.

Carbon accounting is increasingly mandated in manufacturing; however, its implementation remains fragmented between corporate reporting and product‐level assessments, limiting its effectiveness for decarbonisation.
