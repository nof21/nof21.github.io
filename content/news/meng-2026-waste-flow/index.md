Our paper “Waste flow and environmental analysis of municipal solid waste management in England” has been published in *Environmental Nexus*, with Fanran Meng, Lukas Gast, Sylvanus Lilonfe, Jon McKechnie, Takuma Watari, Caitlyn Haigh and Jonathan Cullen.

The UK generated 26.4 million tonnes of household waste in 2019, corresponding to 12% of total waste generation. England was responsible for 83% of this household waste, and just 46.2% of it was recycled.
