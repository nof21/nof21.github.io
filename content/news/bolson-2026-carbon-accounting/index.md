Our paper “Navigating Carbon Accounting in Manufacturing From Compliance to Decarbonisation” has been published in *Business Strategy and the Environment*, with Alexandra Rosioru, Marie Wells and Justyna Rybicka.

The paper examines how manufacturers can move carbon accounting beyond regulatory compliance and use it to support decarbonisation.
