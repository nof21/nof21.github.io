Our paper “Waste flow and environmental analysis of municipal solid waste management in England”, led by Fanran Meng, has been published in *Environmental Nexus*, with Lukas Gast, Sylvanus Lilonfe, Jon McKechnie, Takuma Watari, Caitlyn Haigh and Jonathan Cullen.

Combining material flow analysis and life cycle assessment of government waste data, the study maps where municipal solid waste in England is generated and treated, estimates the emissions from treating it, and shows how waste management varies between regions.
