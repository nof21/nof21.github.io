Our paper “Empirical evidence of improvements in material and carbon efficiency of crystalline silicon solar panels” has been published in *Materials Research Express*, with Haider Iqbal.

 Using specifications from 320 commercial modules, we compare panels sold in 2008–2012 with those sold in 2020–2024.
