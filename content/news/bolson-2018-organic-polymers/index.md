Our paper “All Organic Polymers Based Morphing Skin with Controllable Surface Texture” has been published in *University of Birmingham Research Portal (University of Birmingham)*.

Smart skins are integrating an increasing number of functionalities in order to improve the interaction between the systems they equip and their ambient environment. Here we have developed an electromechanical soft actuator with controlled surface texture due to applied thermal gradient via electrical voltage.
