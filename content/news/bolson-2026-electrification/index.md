Our paper “Industrial energy efficiency and decarbonisation via electrification and hydrogen” has been published in *Applied Energy*, with Takuma Watari, Richard Boocock and Jonathan Cullen.

 We develop a process-level energy allocation framework for UK industry and compare direct electrification, hydrogen substitution and a hybrid pathway.
