Our paper “Material circularity in the UK's foundation industries” has been published in *Resources, Conservation and Recycling*, with Masoud Ahmadinia, Rossi Setchi, Sam Evans and Jonathan Cullen.

 The study evaluates how circular economy strategies could change the use of aluminium, lead, steel, glass and paper in UK industry.
